tui-view plain-text sample

This file is rendered by the plain-text view. Source line breaks are kept, so
this paragraph stays one logical line and gets soft-wrapped to whatever width
the terminal gives it — resize the window and the wrapping follows.

    Indented lines and    runs of spaces are preserved as written.

Blank lines above and below stay blank. Nothing here is styled; it is your
content, verbatim, just made scrollable.
